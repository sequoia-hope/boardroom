# Chip: RP2350 (RP2350A QFN-60, RP2350B QFN-80)

Load this file on any board with an RP2350. Its power copper is not ours to invent. Raspberry Pi publishes a layout and says to follow it ("this should be placed first on any board design and these guidelines must be strictly followed", RP2350 datasheet §6.3.8). The user has had to say "use the reference design's power planes" more than once (bluebuzz-rm2, 2026-10-18). Treat that as a standing rule.

## Sources (get them before placing anything)

- **Vendor KiCad files:** "RP2350A Minimal Board" (RP-006440-DD) and "RP2350B Minimal Board" (RP-006442-DD), from the *Hardware design with RP2350* page.
  - Copy the `.kicad_pcb`, the `.kicad_pro` and the `.pretty` folder into the project's `design/reference/`.
- **Raspberry Pi Pico 2:** the same regulator layout, in Allegro or KiCad.
- **RP2350 datasheet §6.3.8,** "External components and PCB layout requirements": the regulator rules below.
- **Hardware design with RP2350,** "Power" chapter: decoupling and the inductor's polarity.

## What to copy, and how

**Transplant, don't redraw.** Read the vendor `.kicad_pcb` by script and copy the power block relative to U1's centre:
- footprint positions and rotations;
- zone outlines with all their settings: priority, pad connection, clearance, minimum width, thermal gap and spoke;
- the vias and tracks of the power nets.

The QFN footprints match KiCad's stock QFN-60-1EP_7x7_P0.4mm_EP3.4x3.4mm pin for pin, so the copied geometry fits by translation. A rotated U1 needs the rotation applied too. A chip on B.Cu mirrors Y (`(dx, −dy)`) and puts this copper on B.Cu.

Map the parts by function, and check that pad 1 carries the same net as in the reference before reusing a rotation.

Never let a router redistribute 1V1 or IOVDD as thin wires: that is the failure this note exists for. Bring the vendor's **zones** across, placed relative to the chip, and place the **pre-route copper** below before the first routing trial:
- the under-chip 1V1 ring and 3V3 band zones, and the four regulator zones (`VREG_LX`, `1V1`, `3V3`, `PGND`), each with an explicit clearance (`pcbnew.ZONE()` defaults to 0.5 mm);
- the short track stubs joining the 3V3 pins to the 3V3 band, locked so `autoroute.py`'s `--strip` keeps them and via planners avoid them (never draw pin stubs or pin-to-cap links as thin strip zones: vias carve them into slivers);
- PGND's two adjacent vias at (3.1, −4.9) and (3.1, −5.5), and CFILT's separate GND via;
- the LX plane cutout and the ring / regulator-corner rule areas.

Zones and locked pre-route stubs survive `--strip`; the router lays the remaining unlocked tracks and vias around them.

### The power block on the RP2350A minimal board (relative to U1's centre, mm)

**Under the chip, chip layer:**
- **1V1 ring.** Zone ±2.2 square, priority 3, thermal connection, minimum width 0.2. It fills the gap between the GND exposed pad and the pin row.
- **3V3 band.** Zone ±2.8 square, priority 1, solid connection. It stops short of the QFN-60 pads: pad inner ends sit at ±3.00 mm on the vendor footprint (0.20 mm gap) and ±3.05 mm on KiCad's stock QFN-60 (0.25 mm gap), so the band touches no pad on its own. Join each 3V3 pin to the band before the first routing trial:
  - IOVDD pins 11, 20, 30, 38 and pins 53 (USB_OTP_VDD), 54 (QSPI_IOVDD): short 0.20 mm wide track stubs from pad centre (±3.44 mm) into the band (±2.60 mm, 0.84 mm long; −2.70 mm, 0.74 mm long, at pins 53/54; pin 30 doglegs inward from (2.80, 3.44) to (2.42, 2.60)).
  - Pin 49 (VREG_VIN): joined by the priority-7 3V3 CIN zone (`x = 1.25..1.70, y = −4.85..−2.70`), or a 0.15–0.20 mm stub (`y = −3.44 → −2.60`, 0.84 mm long) if that zone is not yet placed.
  - Corner IOVDD pins 1 and 45 (and 44, ADC_AVDD): fed from outside by the vendor's outer 3V3 pour; without an outer 3V3 pour, add 0.15 mm wide stubs from pad centre to the band (`±3.44 → ±2.60`, 0.84 mm long, 0.125 mm clear of the adjacent corner pin).
- **RP2350B QFN-80.** Pad inner ends sit at ±4.51 mm on the vendor footprint and ±4.55 mm on KiCad's stock `QFN-80-1EP_10x10mm_P0.4mm_EP3.4x3.4mm`. The 1V1 ring is ±2.6 mm and the vendor's inner 3V3 band stops at ±4.1–4.2 mm (0.31–0.41 mm short of the pads). Join inner 3V3 pins (5, 15, 24, 29, 50, 64, 68, 69, 76) to the band with 0.20 mm wide stubs (`±4.90 → ±4.10`, ~0.80 mm long; to +3.80 at pin 50), and join corner pins (41, 59, 60) either from an outer 3V3 pour or with inward stubs.

**1V1 spokes.** Four 0.6/0.25 vias in the ring: N (0.49, −2.15), S (0.20, 2.15), W (−2.15, −0.80), E (2.15, −0.40). From each, a 0.6 mm wire runs on the far side to a via beside a DVDD pin or the regulator output:
- N → (0.5, −5.8) and (0.5, −5.2), at COUT;
- S → (0.2, 5.8), at pin 23 and its 4.7 µF;
- W → (−5.2, −1.0), at pin 6;
- E → (5.8, −0.4), at pin 39.

Each DVDD pin then takes a 0.2 mm top-layer trace to its spoke via and its capacitor.

**Regulator, above pins 46–50, all on the top side:**

| Part | Role | Position | Rotation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | 3.3 µH, LX pad on the right | (2.0, −7.2) | 0 |
| C7 | COUT | (2.0, −5.55) | 0 |
| C6 | CIN | (2.0, −4.6) | 0 |
| C9 | CFILT | (4.2, −5.05) | −90 |
| R5 | RFILT 33 Ω | (4.2, −6.9) | −90 |

C6 and C7 use 0402 "small pads".

The high-current paths are small top-layer zones:
- VREG_LX from pin 48 to the inductor's LX pad (priority 5);
- 1V1 from the inductor to COUT and the N spoke vias (priority 6);
- 3V3 from pin 49 to CIN (priority 7);
- PGND from pin 47 to the CIN/COUT grounds (priority 4), taken to the GND plane at ONE point through two adjacent vias at (3.1, −4.9) and (3.1, −5.5).

VREG_FB runs from pin 50 to COUT, not under LX. VREG_AVDD runs from pin 46 to C9/R5.

**USB, pins 51/52:** the 27 Ω resistors sit at (−1.1, −7.4) and (−0.1, −7.4), rotation −90. Pins 53/54 share one 100 nF at (−2.1, −7.4), rotation 90.

### Datasheet rules the copy must keep (§6.3.8.1)

- CIN, L1 and COUT are on the same side as the chip. Never on the back.
- Cut away the copper on the plane layer next to the chip's side (In1.Cu for a chip on F.Cu; the inner layer next to B.Cu for a chip on B.Cu) beneath the LX node. Cover the VREG_LX zone and L1's LX pad with a rule area on that layer.
- On the chip's layer, no other copper under the inductor. Cut back copper near the VREG_LX trace by giving its zone a larger clearance.
- PGND joins the main GND at one point, through two adjacent vias. CFILT gets its own GND path; it must not share the CIN/COUT GND vias.
- A second 4.7 µF on DVDD pin 23 (bottom edge), away from LX and COUT.
- Inductor: the polarity-marked Abracon AOTA-B201610S3R3-101-T, in Raspberry Pi's `L_pol_2016` footprint, at the reference rotation. Orientation changes the regulator's output under load.
  JLC stock of it is thin (C42411119: 191 then 1 on 2026-10-23). Check it at phase 1 and again at release, and plan on JLC global sourcing or a pre-order of the same MPN; don't substitute.

### Lessons from the easy2350 example (21 mm wide, 4 layers)

- `scripts/transplant.py` does the copy, into a scratch copy of the board;
  `board.py regzones` keeps its zones and the six 0.20 mm 3V3 band stubs. Box it
  to the vendor's decoupler ring: the vendor board is wider, and its outermost
  GND vias land on a Pico-width board's header pads.
- The vendor feeds IOVDD pins 1 and 45 (ring corners), VREG_VIN (49) and CIN
  from a large outer 3V3 pour. Without that pour they are islands: join each
  to the 3V3 band with a 0.15 mm stub (0.125 mm from the next corner pin) and
  run pin 1 on to its decoupler, as the vendor does for pin 11.
- Copy the USB pin-to-27 Ω routing too (`Net-(U1-USB_DP)` / `_DM`): the
  autorouter crosses the pair.
- Keep the vendor's escape directions free: an extra cap at pin 44 blocked
  GPIO29; R8/TP3 on the diagonal GPIO16 takes from pin 27 blocked it.
- Inside the pad ring and over the regulator corner (L1's body), no foreign
  net: named rule areas that restrict nothing, and `.kicad_dru` rules that
  disallow tracks and vias of every other net there (`A.intersectsArea(…)`),
  exempting the corner's own nets (VREG_LX, 1V1; GND, 1V1, 3V3 in the ring).
  A plain tracks keepout also blocks the regulator's own copper from L1's pads.
  After routing: cut the outer GND pour around the regulator corner and L1
  (no other copper under the inductor; PGND joins GND only at its two vias).

### Checks

- Right after building the chip's zones (before the first routing trial), refill zones and check band-to-pin copper connectivity: each IOVDD pin's pad (plus VREG_VIN, USB_OTP_VDD and QSPI_IOVDD) touches 3V3 copper.
- After routing, every RP2350 power zone is present, filled, and connected to its pins.
- No foreign net crosses the area inside the pad ring.
- 1V1 is never a thin wire between the spoke vias and the ring.
- The cutout on the plane layer next to the chip's side (In1.Cu for a chip on F.Cu; the inner layer next to B.Cu for a chip on B.Cu) is present under LX.
- Render the regulator corner beside the reference and compare.
