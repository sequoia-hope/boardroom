# Gate 1 independent review — USB front end, 3V3 LDO, QSPI flash

Reviewer scope: `design/parts/usb-front-end.md`, `ldo-3v3.md`, `flash.md`. Reviewed 2026-10-23
against `design/intent.md`, the netlist exported from `hw/easy2350/a/easy2350.kicad_sch`
(`kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml`), the datasheets in `design/datasheets/`
(re-read: USBLC6-2 Rev 5 all pages; AP7361C DS37274 Rev 5-2 pp.2, 4–6, 12–15, 19, 21; W25Q128JV
Rev J pp.6, 17, 61–65, 68, 75; RP2350 datasheet Tables 1675/1678; hardware guide §5.1; the HRO
drawing rendered at 300 and 600 dpi), the KiCad 9 system libraries under `/usr/share/kicad/`,
and `jlc_parts.py show` run today. Every calculation below was redone from the datasheet
numbers, not copied from the audit. USB / Type-C specification numbers are **from memory**
(no spec in `datasheets/`) and are marked "(spec, from memory)".

Severity: BLOCKER = cannot pass the gate; MAJOR = must be fixed or explicitly waived before
the gate passes; MINOR = fix at the next convenient phase, log it; NOTE = verified or recorded.

## Verdict for this scope

No BLOCKER. Two MAJORs, both schematic-completeness items that take minutes: add the 3V3 test
point (#1) and label the connector-side USB pair to match `nets.csv` (#2). Everything the three
audits claim from the datasheets checked out page for page; the two calculation corrections
(#5 tempco against junction temperature, #6 clamp level is an assumption) do not change a part.
Recommended while the schematic is open: C2 → 2.2 µF (#9).
