# Decisions

Every non-obvious decision, logged as it is made. Newest at the bottom.

| Date | Decision | Why | Alternatives considered |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-22 | Example board is an RP2350A dev board in the Pico's 21 × 51 mm outline, not pin-compatible | Shows the RP2350 chip note, USB-C and a real gate flow on a board people want; exposing all 30 GPIO is more useful than Pico pin-compatibility | Pico-compatible pinout (hides 4 GPIO, needs 3V3_EN/ADC_VREF); a simpler LED board (too little to show) |
| 2026-10-22 | LDO from VBUS, not a buck | ≤ 330 mA at 3V3 keeps loss ≤ 0.65 W, which a SOT-223/DFN LDO handles at 40 °C; fewer parts, no switching noise next to the RP2350's own regulator | Buck or the Pico's RT6150 buck-boost: more parts and layout for no gain at this load |
| 2026-10-23 | Four addressable RGB LEDs on GPIO23, powered from VBUS; header limits cut to 100 mA (3V3) and 50 mA (VBUS) | User asked for addressable LEDs. At full white they draw 160 mA, which only fits the 500 mA USB default if the header outputs give it up; the 3V3 cut also lowers the LDO's loss | LEDs on 3V3 (LDO loss 0.9 W and blue/green dim at 3.3 V); keep header limits and exceed 500 mA (not USB-compliant); fewer LEDs |
| 2026-10-23 | Run the example's later user-sign-off gates (4, 10) without stopping | User: "proceed with development"; the example is a demonstration and the user reviews the result at the end | Stop at each gate |
| 2026-10-23 | GPIO headers J2/J3 mount from the bottom (footprints on B side) | That is how the board is used: plastic under the board, pins into a breadboard, as on a Pico. | 2026-10-23 | VBUS, 3V3 and GND test points are header pins 40, 36, 38; only 1V1 gets a pad (TP3) | Phase 4 area budget: the bottom block needed ~141 mm² of courtyard in ~142 mm². A header pin is a better probe point than a 1.5 mm pad | Four pads (doesn't fit) |

| 2026-10-23 | SW1/SW2 TS-1088-AR02016 (3.9 × 3 mm, basic) instead of TS-1187A (5.1 mm) | Space: two 7.6 mm courtyards can't sit beside the pixels | Side-actuated switches (no free edge: headers on both long edges) |

| 2026-10-23 | C2 (LDO input) 1 µF → 2.2 µF 16 V X5R 0603 | Meets the USB 2.0 ≥ 1 µF across VBUS after tolerance and DC bias; VBUS total 7.5 µF stays under 10 µF (review1-power #9) | 1 µF (marginal on the letter) |
| 2026-10-23 | U3 sits 0.7 mm from J1's body, not ≥ 5 mm (ldo-3v3.md layout guidance) | VBUS enters at J1 and the only free 4 × 4 mm near it is beside J1. | 2026-10-23 | L3 is a whole-board 3V3 plane; signals route on L1 and L4 only | First routing used L3 for signals and added a partial 3V3 pour afterwards: Freerouting never dropped vias to it, leaving 3V3 islands, while L4 sat nearly empty. | 2026-10-23 | No separate ADC_AVDD capacitor: C9 serves pins 44 and 45, as the vendor's C17 does (rp2350-core.md listed C_ADC44) | Placed at pin 44, it sat in front of pin 43 and blocked GPIO29's escape; the vendor reference board decouples 44/45 with one 100 nF | Keep C10 elsewhere (no room within 3 mm of pin 44 that isn't an escape path) |
| 2026-10-23 | No SWD series resistors (rp2350-core.md suggested 100 Ω on SWCLK/SWDIO) | Phase 7: they, D1/R9 and TP3 formed a wall across the right-side channel GPIO16-22 need, and the router could not finish. | 2026-10-23 | D1/R9 moved to the top-right beside J3 pin 13; TP3 is a 1.0 mm pad | Same channel; GPIO25 runs to J3 pin 13 anyway. A 1.0 mm pad still takes a probe tip | — |
